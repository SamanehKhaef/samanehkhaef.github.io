About

I am a Human geographer and Postdoctoral Researcher at the Department of Human Geography, Stockholm University.

My research focuses on migration and integration, education and labour-market pathways, residential mobility, housing and neighbourhoods, regional and geographical inequalities, spatial and urban–rural contexts, life-course trajectories and integration outcomes.

I have extensive teaching experience across Bachelor's and Master's courses in human geography, GIS, spatial analysis, migration, research methods, as well as supervision.

Research Interests

  • Migration and integration
  • Education and labour-market integration
  • Residential choice and mobility
  • Housing, neighbourhoods and segregation
  • Spatial inequality and regional contexts
  • Integration across generations and across the urban–rural hierarchy
  • Kinship, social ties and residential pathways
  • School and preschool segregation and educational pathways

Beyond Academia

Being from Iran, I have always been inspired by art, poetry, and the beauty of Persian miniature. After moving to Sweden, I found that miniature painting became a way of carrying a sense of home with me. It has given me a creative space to reconnect with familiar colours, stories, and traditions while creating something new.

Persian miniature is a traditional form of detailed painting, characterised by intricate patterns, delicate figures, vivid colours, and storytelling. I started learning and practising it as a hobby, and it has gradually become a meaningful part of my life beyond academia.
