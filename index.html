About

I am a Human geographer and Postdoctoral Researcher at the Department of Human Geography, Stockholm University.

My research focuses on migration and integration, education and labour-market pathways, residential mobility, housing and neighbourhoods, regional and geographical inequalities, spatial and urban–rural contexts, and life-course trajectories and integration outcomes.

I also have extensive teaching experience across Bachelor's and Master's courses in human geography, GIS, spatial analysis, migration, research methods, as well as student supervision.

Research Interests

  • Migration and integration
  • Education and labour-market integration
  • Residential choice and mobility
  • Housing, neighbourhoods and segregation
  • Spatial inequality and regional contexts
  • Integration across generations and across the urban–rural hierarchy
  • Kinship, social ties and residential pathways
  • School and preschool segregation and educational pathways
