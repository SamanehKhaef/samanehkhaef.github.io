About

I am a Human geographer and Postdoctoral Researcher at the Department of Human Geography, Stockholm University.

My research focuses on migration and integration, education and labour-market pathways, residential mobility, housing and neighbourhoods, with particular attention to regional and geographical inequalities and how spatial and urban–rural contexts shape life-course trajectories and integration outcomes.

I have extensive teaching experience across Bachelor's and Master's courses in human geography, GIS, spatial analysis, migration, and research methods, as well as experience supervising students at both levels.

Research

My research interests include:

  • Migration and integration
  • Education and labour-market integration
  • Residential choice and mobility
  • Housing, neighbourhoods and segregation
  • Spatial inequality and regional contexts
  • Integration across generations and across the urban–rural hierarchy
  • Kinship, social ties and residential pathways
  • School and preschool segregation and educational pathways

Teaching & Supervision

I have taught undergraduate and postgraduate courses at Stockholm University since 2018, including courses in GIS and spatial analysis, geographical methods, migration, Swedish geography, geographical information analysis, and place, identity and migration.

At postgraduate level, I have taught Migration and Social Change – A Life Course Perspective and Advanced Method in Human Geography and Urban and Regional Planning.

I have also supervised Bachelor's and Master's students in areas including migration, labour-market integration, language and integration, urban environments, and accessibility.
